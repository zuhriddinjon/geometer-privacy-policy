This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app GeoMeter: Land Area Measure (the "App"), published on Google Play by the developer Doston ("we", "us"), accesses, collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the App. It applies only to this App.

1. Information the App accesses or collects

DataWhenPurpose
Precise and approximate location (GPS) Only after you grant location permission and while you measure. When you walk a boundary, a foreground service with a visible notification keeps recording points while the screen is off. To place points on the map and calculate land area, perimeter and distance. Location is processed on your device and is not sent to us, except as the points of measurements you choose to back up (see "Cloud backup").
Saved measurements (name, points/coordinates, area, distance, dates) When you save a measurement. Stored locally on your device. Uploaded only if you turn on Cloud backup.
Google account details (email address, account identifier) Only if you choose "Sign in with Google" for Cloud backup. To identify your backup and sync your measurements across your devices.
Support messages (the text you write, and the name and phone number if you choose to enter them) Only when you contact us through the in-app support chat. To answer your question and keep the conversation history.
Device and app information (device brand and model, Android version, app version, Android device ID, push notification token) Sent together with support chat messages. To identify your conversation, diagnose problems and notify you when we reply.
App usage and diagnostics (app interactions such as screens opened and features used, chosen settings such as language, theme and unit, crash logs, performance data, app instance identifiers) Automatically while you use the App. To understand which features are used, fix crashes and improve the App.

The App does not collect your contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone or camera data, and it does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. The App does not show ads.

2. Third-party services

The App uses the following services provided by Google, which process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Maps SDK for Android – map display (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play services Location – GPS location
  • Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In / Credential Manager – optional sign-in for Cloud backup
  • Cloud Firestore – storage of backed-up measurements and support chat messages
  • Google Analytics for Firebase – usage statistics
  • Firebase Crashlytics – crash reports
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase In-App Messaging and Firebase Remote Config – notifications, in-app announcements and configuration

See Privacy and Security in Firebase for details. Apart from these service providers, we do not share your data with third parties, unless required by law.

3. Cloud backup

Cloud backup is optional. If you sign in with Google, your saved measurements are stored in Cloud Firestore under your account so they can be restored and synced. Each account can access only its own data. You can sign out at any time in Settings; your measurements then stay only on your device.

4. Data security

All data sent by the App is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to stored data is restricted by security rules and limited to the developer for support purposes.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Measurements stored on your device are deleted when you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Backed-up measurements and support chat history are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period (analytics data up to 14 months, crash data up to 90 days).

To delete your account, your backed-up measurements or your support chat history, email zuhriddinjonrayimjonov@gmail.com with the subject "GeoMeter data deletion" and the Google account email you used (or describe your support conversation). We will delete the data within 30 days.

6. Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke the location permission in Android settings; measuring by GPS will then not be possible, but you can still place points on the map manually.
  • You can disable notifications in Android settings.
  • You do not have to sign in or use the support chat to use the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
